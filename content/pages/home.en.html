Welcome to the Valgamaa Business Club!

Since 1994, Valgamaa Äriklubi has brought together business leaders from Valga County to share experience, visit local companies and meet interesting guests.

About the club

The club was founded in September 1994 as the Valga Entrepreneurs’ Club (Valga Ettevõtjate Klubi). In 1995 it was joined by the Valga Chamber of Businessmen (Valga Ärimeeste Koda), and the merged club took the name Valgamaa Äriklubi, the Valgamaa Business Club.

In January 1996 the club was officially registered with the Valga County Government, and since February 1999 it has been registered as a non-profit association (MTÜ).

The club is led by a three-member board. Hans Heinjärv has served as chairman of the board, the club’s president, throughout its history.

Our members are senior executives of the most active companies in Valga County. Today the club brings together more than 40 business leaders from the region.
