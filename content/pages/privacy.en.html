This privacy policy explains what personal data the Valgamaa Äriklubi website collects and how it is used.

Data controller

Valgamaa Äriklubi MTÜ
Kesk 11, 68203 Valga, Estonia
Email: ariklubi@valgamaa.ee

Cookies and analytics

  • This website does not use analytics, advertising or marketing cookies, and visitors are not tracked.
  • The website does not load third-party content (such as Google Fonts, social media buttons or embedded videos); all files are served from our own server.
  • A technical cookie, pll_language, may be set to remember your language choice. It contains no personal data.
  • WordPress login cookies are used only for site administrators who log in. They are not set for ordinary visitors.

Server logs

The website is hosted by Zone Media OÜ (Estonia). The server keeps technical logs (IP address, time, requested address and browser details) to keep the website secure and to troubleshoot problems. Logs are kept for a limited time and are not used to profile visitors. For security, the IP addresses of failed login attempts are also recorded.

Published information

The members page lists the names, companies and websites of club members. The gallery contains photos from club events. If you would like your details or a photo removed or corrected, please contact us.

Contacting us by email

If you contact us by email, we use your details only to reply to your enquiry.

Your rights

You have the right to access the data held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to its processing. To exercise these rights, email ariklubi@valgamaa.ee. You may also lodge a complaint with the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (www.aki.ee).
