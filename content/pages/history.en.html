Valgamaa Äriklubi began in September 1994 with an organisational meeting at the Kungla bar in Valga. The club was originally called the Valga Entrepreneurs’ Club (Valga Ettevõtjate Klubi). In 1995 the Valga Chamber of Businessmen (Valga Ärimeeste Koda) joined, and the merged club took the name Valgamaa Äriklubi.

In January 1996 the club was officially registered with the Valga County Government, and since February 1999 it has been registered as a non-profit association (MTÜ). The club is led by a three-member board, whose chairman, the club’s president, has been Hans Heinjärv. A new board was elected in November 2024.

During the season, from September to May, the club usually meets once a month. Meetings include visits to companies in Valga County and neighbouring regions, talks by guest speakers and training sessions. The club’s guests have included Prime Ministers Tiit Vähi, Mart Siimann, Andrus Ansip, Taavi Rõivas and Jüri Ratas, and in 2010 the club held a business lunch with President Toomas Hendrik Ilves.

The club maintains good relations with the business clubs of neighbouring Võru, Põlva and Viljandi, and with Valka and Valmiera across the border in Latvia. Club anniversaries were celebrated in 2009 (15th), 2014 (20th), 2019 (25th) and 2024 (30th).
